Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Burgonomics for food ordering, delivery, and dine-in services, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number (for OTP authentication and transactional SMS).
  • Profile Information: Name, delivery addresses, and dietary preferences.
  • Order & Transaction Data: Ordered items, customizations, and payment reference IDs.
  • Device Data: IP address, device platform, and push notification tokens.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • Fulfilling and delivering food orders accurately.
  • Transmitting Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) to store POS terminals.
  • Processing transactions securely via RBI-authorized payment gateways (Razorpay).
  • Sending real-time order tracking notifications.

3. Payment & Financial Security

Burgonomics does not store credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank account credentials. All online payments are handled directly by Razorpay in compliance with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, you have the right to access your data, request correction of inaccuracies, and request complete erasure of your account and personal records. You can request deletion directly within the mobile app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing our Data Protection Officer.
