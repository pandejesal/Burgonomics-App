1. Ordering & Acceptance

By placing an order through the Burgonomics mobile application or web portal, you agree to enter into a binding contract for the purchase and delivery of freshly prepared food items. Orders are subject to kitchen capacity and ingredient availability.

2. Pricing & Payments

All prices displayed in the app are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes (GST) unless explicitly noted at checkout. Online payments are securely processed through Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking) or via Pay at Counter / Cash on Delivery where permitted.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

Orders may be cancelled free of charge prior to kitchen confirmation. Once food preparation has begun, cancellations cannot be accepted due to perishable food safety regulations. If an order arrives damaged or incorrect, a full or partial refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days via Razorpay.

4. Delivery Policy

Estimated delivery times are provided in real time based on kitchen load and distance. While our delivery partners strive for punctuality, delivery times may vary due to severe weather or traffic conditions.
