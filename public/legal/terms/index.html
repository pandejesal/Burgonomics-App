The contract between you and Burgonomics when you order through the customer app: acceptance, pricing, payment, cancellation, refunds and the statutory seller disclosure.

Ordering and acceptance

Placing an order through the Burgonomics app is an offer to buy the items at the prices shown. The contract forms when the outlet accepts the order and raises its kitchen ticket. Until then the order may be refused — for example when an item has sold out, or when no courier can serve your address — and the app tells you.

Pricing, taxes and delivery

All prices are in Indian Rupees and already include GST at 5% (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%). GST is shown on your bill for information and is never added on top. Checkout shows the full breakup: item total, less any coupon discount, plus packaging and any delivery fee, plus an optional rider tip if you add one — that is the amount you pay. The packaging charge is set by the outlet. The delivery fee is a live price from our delivery partner for your address and order, shown before you pay. If no delivery partner can serve your address, delivery is not available for that order. We deliver within India only.

Payment methods

  • Online: card, UPI, netbanking or wallet through the Razorpay checkout the app opens.
  • Cash on delivery: pay the rider in cash when the order arrives.
  • Cash at the counter: walk-in and takeaway orders paid at the outlet counter, including orders staff place for a walk-in customer.

A cash order is confirmed when the outlet accepts it, not when cash changes hands. An online order does not reach the kitchen until the payment gateway confirms the payment.

Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel free of charge from the app while your order is being prepared or earlier, and before a rider has been assigned. That is the window in which neither the kitchen nor a courier has committed to your order. Once a rider is assigned, or the order is ready, picked up or out for delivery, the app cannot cancel it — call the outlet from the order screen or raise a support ticket, and the store decides what can still be done. The full rules, refund routes and timelines are in the Refund & Cancellation Policy, which forms part of these terms.

The cancellation action is server-side: it re-checks ownership and the window before writing anything, so a second tap or a second device cannot double-cancel.
