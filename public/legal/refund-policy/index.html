Refund & Cancellation Policy

PANCHTATVA FOODS (trading as Burgonomics) · Effective 20 November 2026

When you can cancel, what happens once a rider is assigned, and how money comes back for online, cash-on-delivery and counter-cash orders.

When you can cancel in the app

You may cancel from the app while your order is being prepared or earlier — in other words, before a rider has been assigned to it. That covers the time before the kitchen commits food and before a courier commits a rider; normally the first few minutes after you order.

  • No fee is charged for a cancellation inside that window.
  • Once a rider is assigned, or the order is ready, picked up or out for delivery, the app refuses the cancellation and says so. Call the outlet from the order screen or raise a support ticket; the store decides what can still be done.
  • A cancelled order is cancelled on the server, not only on your phone, so the kitchen and the courier see the same state.

The app also shows a short free-cancellation countdown right after you order. That countdown is a convenience display; the rule that decides a cancellation is the one above, checked on the server at the moment you tap.

Refunds for online payments

When a paid order is cancelled, a refund request is raised against the captured payment and returned to the original payment method through Razorpay. A refund is released after the store reviews the request — a deliberate human check, not an automatic payout — and once released it usually reaches your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

  • Partial refunds are possible where only part of the order is affected.
  • If a payment is captured but the order cannot be created, or is cancelled before payment confirmation, the amount is returned in full.
  • A refund cannot be redirected to a different card, UPI ID or bank account; it always returns to the method that paid.

Cash on delivery and counter cash

A cash-on-delivery order cancelled before dispatch has taken no money, so there is nothing to refund. If cash was already collected and the order cannot be delivered, the outlet refunds the cash directly — the rider or the store hands it back and the order record notes it.

Counter cash, for walk-in and takeaway orders paid at the outlet, is refunded at the counter by the outlet. The app records the payment but cannot move counter cash back to you.

Missing, wrong or spoiled items

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete or incorrect, raise a support ticket from the order screen and attach photos where you can. The store reviews the ticket, and a full or partial refund follows the route above depending on how the order was paid.
